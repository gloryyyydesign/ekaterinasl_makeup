05 — DESIGN RATIONALE

Why It Works

01

Editorial
Discipline

A single hero portrait, a clean typographic hierarchy, and restraint at every touchpoint build the kind of trust a premium beauty professional needs. No clutter — only what earns the booking.

02

Three Audiences,
One Voice

EN / PL / RU language switching is instant, saved per-user, and invisible to anyone who doesn't need it. The entire Warsaw expat and local market is served from a single URL with zero friction.

03

Restrained
Contrast

Warm cream backgrounds, a single rose accent family, and deep neutral darks align perfectly with the brand's premium positioning. The palette does the selling before a word is read.

06 — BEFORE / AFTER

What Changed

Before
— No web presence — clients found via Instagram only
— No price transparency — DMs clogged with basic questions
— No multilingual reach — RU / EN leads difficult to convert
— Services & brow tariffs never publicly documented
— Training income channel untapped digitally
— Portfolio scattered across Instagram stories
After
+ Full editorial website — live, indexed, shareable link
+ All services & brow tariffs clearly priced on-page
+ EN / PL / RU — all three audiences served from one site
+ 10-case portfolio with category filters for client validation
+ Training section opens a second, season-independent income stream
+ Pre-qualified leads arrive informed — fewer questions, higher check
